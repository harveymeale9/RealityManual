Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Each copy is printed and bound to order, then dispatched from the press nearest you. Most orders arrive within 7–14 days, depending on destination. You'll see your exact shipping cost at checkout.

What is your return policy?

If your copy arrives damaged or misprinted, email us and we'll send a replacement at no cost. Because every book is printed to order, we don't accept change-of-mind returns.

Do you ship to my country?

We ship worldwide. Select your country at checkout and the exact shipping cost is calculated before you pay — no surprises, no customs handling fees added by us.

This is the first edition, printed as a single deluxe run. Any future edition would be a distinct printing — the first edition will not be reproduced in this form.
