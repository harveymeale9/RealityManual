Frequently Asked Questions

Was this book written by AI?

Absolutely not. The original manuscript was written (and rewritten) years before consumer AI tools became publicly available. Some illustrations were indeed designed using AI, however.

What inspired The Reality Manual, and how was it written?

The entire book emerged during the author's early-morning walks in nature. Rather than being consciously developed through research or study, the ideas seemed to arrive spontaneously, almost as though they were being downloaded into the author's mind. Channeled, if you will.

In this sense, the author does not claim ownership of the ideas, but rather sees themselves as a vessel through which they were expressed.

No external research informed the original manuscript. Instead, the concepts are presented as self-evident truths that anyone can independently explore and verify through careful, discerning observation of reality.

Are the 14 Rules original concepts, or a collection of existing ideas?

The 14 Rules are about as original as the metaphysics permits. Some concepts may be entirely new to you, while others may feel familiar or reflect truths that have been recognized and explored for centuries. The book brings these ideas together into a coherent framework for understanding reality.

Who authored The Reality Manual?

The author is an Australian metaphysicist who has spent the past decade exploring the nature of reality and what it means to "exist well" as an incarnate being participating in the game of life.

Although he has written extensively on other subjects, The Reality Manual is his first publicly released work that explores the topic of reality and life strategy.

He has deliberately chosen to maintain a relatively anonymous public presence, partly for reasons explored in Section 9 of the book. Fundamentally, he believes that ideas should be judged on their own merit, rather than the credentials, reputation, or perceived authority of the person presenting them.

Is the author the same person featured in the videos?

Yes. The person appearing in The Reality Manual videos is the author of the book.

Where can I learn more about The Reality Manual?

The best place to explore the ideas further is our YouTube channel, where you'll find long-form videos examining the concepts, principles, and philosophical questions explored in the book.
