The Reality Manual Content Studio

Platform data deletion instructions

Content Studio is a private internal tool used only to manage The Reality Manual's own Facebook Page, Instagram professional account and other first-party publishing channels. It has no public user accounts.

Disconnect and delete authorization data

  1. Sign in to Content Studio as an authorized administrator.
  2. Open Content Settings.
  3. Under Facebook + Instagram · direct, select Disconnect.

Disconnecting deletes the stored Meta access tokens and connected Page and Instagram identifiers from Content Studio. Authorization can also be revoked directly through the connected Facebook or Instagram account's app and website permissions.

Request assistance

An authorized account owner may request deletion of any remaining Meta integration data by emailing support@realitymanual.com. Include “Meta data deletion” in the subject. Requests are verified and completed within 30 days.
