Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

What this is

"Content Studio" is an internal tool built and operated by The Reality Manual (the publisher of the physical book sold at realitymanual.com) for our own small content team to plan, produce, and schedule short- and long-form video content for publishing to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook. It is not a public product — it has no public sign-up, is protected by a single shared password known only to our team, and is used exclusively by authorized members of The Reality Manual's own staff.

Information we process

Because this tool is for our internal team only, it does not collect personal information from the public. What it does hold:

  • Content our own team creates or uploads — video files, audio files, transcripts, captions, notes, and scheduling metadata for our own social media posts
  • Basic session information for our team's own login (a single shared password, checked server-side, and a session cookie — no names, emails, or other personal profiles are stored for our internal users)
  • When a team member connects one of our own social accounts (e.g. via TikTok Login Kit), an access token authorizing this tool to publish content to that account on our behalf, for as long as the connection remains active

How platform integrations work

Where this tool connects to a third-party platform's official API (for example, TikTok's Content Posting API), it does so only to publish content that a member of our own team has personally created and approved, to an account our own team controls and has explicitly authorized via that platform's own OAuth login flow. We never use these integrations to access, post on behalf of, or process data belonging to any third party or end user — only our own account and our own content.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent, or share any information with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We don't run third-party analytics or advertising scripts on this tool.
  • We don't collect information from the public — there is no public-facing signup or account creation.

Security & retention

Access is restricted by password and session cookie. Uploaded files and content records are retained for as long as they're useful to our own content pipeline, or deleted by our team when no longer needed. Platform access tokens are retained only for as long as the corresponding account connection is active, and can be revoked at any time both from within this tool and directly from the connected platform's own account settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy — including from a platform review team — can be sent to support@realitymanual.com.
