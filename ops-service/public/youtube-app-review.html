How Content Studio uses the YouTube Data API

This page describes our app, The Reality Manual — Content Studio, for Google's OAuth verification review. It is an internal production tool used by our own small team to prepare and schedule video content, and to publish that content to our own YouTube channel once we've reviewed and approved it. This page is intentionally public (not behind our internal login) so it can serve as the app's homepage on the OAuth consent screen, per Google's requirement that the homepage be reachable without signing in. Every screen shown below is our real product's actual design system, illustrated with clearly-labeled example data.

01Who we are & what the app does

The Reality Manual is a small publishing business (book sold at realitymanual.com). Content Studio is our own in-house tool for planning, producing, and cross-posting the short- and long-form video content that promotes the book. It already supports a full production pipeline (drag-and-drop video upload, automatic audio transcription, ambient-audio splicing, thumbnail selection, and a human review/approval gate). We're requesting YouTube Data API access so our team can publish an approved video directly to our own channel from inside this tool, instead of uploading it manually through YouTube Studio afterward.

02Connecting our YouTube channel

A team member connects our own YouTube channel once, from Content Studio's Settings panel, via Google's standard OAuth consent screen. We only ever connect a channel The Reality Manual itself owns — this tool has no concept of a third-party or end user at all, and the connect button lives inside our authenticated internal panel (Content Studio is not a public product; see §07 below), so the OAuth grant itself is always performed by an authorized member of our own team.

03Producing & reviewing the video (already built, platform-agnostic)

This is the part of Content Studio that already exists today for TikTok/Instagram/Facebook, and that YouTube posting plugs straight into. A team member drops a finished video into the tool; it's automatically transcribed, matched to a title, and spliced with backing audio. Nothing is ever posted anywhere until a human reviews it on our Final Check screen and explicitly approves it — there is no auto-posting path in this product.

04Scheduling & publishing

Once approved, the post moves to our existing scheduling queue, which already assigns a publish time per platform's posting cadence. At the scheduled time, our backend calls the YouTube Data API's videos.insert endpoint using the connected channel's OAuth token to publish the video — the same "nothing is claimed successful until the platform actually confirms it" pattern our storefront already uses for order fulfillment elsewhere in this codebase.

05Scopes we're requesting, and why

ScopeWhy we need itWhy not narrower
youtube.upload To publish an approved video directly to our connected channel on the schedule our team sets, instead of uploading it manually. This is already the narrowest scope the YouTube Data API offers for publishing a video.
youtube.readonly To look up the connected channel's own name, so Settings can show which channel is connected ("Connected as …") before any video is ever published. We only ever read our own channel's basic identity, never any other channel's data, comments, or analytics.

We do not request youtube.force-ssl or the full youtube scope, since neither is needed for what this tool actually does (upload our own videos, show which channel is connected).

06Compliance commitments

  • Every post is created and approved by a human member of our own team — there is no automated content generation or auto-posting.
  • We only ever post to our own connected YouTube channel, never on behalf of a third party.
  • We display the connected channel and a full content preview before every post, and require explicit approval each time.
  • OAuth tokens are stored server-side only, are never exposed to the browser, and can be revoked at any time both from within this tool and directly from a user's Google Account permissions page.
  • We will not request scopes beyond youtube.upload and youtube.readonly unless our use of the product genuinely expands to need them.

07Access & legal

Content Studio itself is not a public product: it has no public sign-up and is used exclusively by authorized members of The Reality Manual's own staff, behind a single shared password. This page, and our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service (both linked below), are deliberately public so Google's review process can read them without needing our internal credentials. Our Privacy Policy specifically covers how we access, use, store, and share Google user data obtained through this integration.
