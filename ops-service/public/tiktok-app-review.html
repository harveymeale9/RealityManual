How Content Studio uses the TikTok Content Posting API

This page walks through exactly how our app, The Reality Manual — Content Studio, integrates with TikTok. It's an internal production tool used by our own small team to prepare and schedule short-form video content, and to publish that content to our own TikTok account once we've reviewed and approved it. Every screen below is our real product's actual design system, shown here with clearly-labeled example data so this page doesn't require a login or expose any of our real content pipeline.

01Who we are & what the app does

The Reality Manual is a small publishing business (book sold at realitymanual.com). Content Studio is our own in-house tool for planning, producing, and cross-posting the short-form video content that promotes the book — think a lightweight, purpose-built alternative to a generic social scheduling SaaS, built for our own workflow. It already supports a full production pipeline (drag-and-drop video upload, automatic audio transcription, ambient-audio splicing, thumbnail selection, and a human review/approval gate) publishing to YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook. We're requesting TikTok Content Posting API access to add TikTok as a fourth destination in that same, already-built pipeline.

02Connecting our TikTok account

A team member connects our own TikTok account once, from Content Studio's Settings panel, via TikTok's Login Kit OAuth flow. We only ever connect an account that The Reality Manual itself owns or has been explicitly authorized to post on behalf of — this tool has no concept of a third-party or end user at all.

03Producing & reviewing the video (already built, platform-agnostic)

This is the part of Content Studio that already exists today for YouTube/Instagram/Facebook, and that TikTok posting will plug straight into. A team member drops a finished video into the tool; it's automatically transcribed, matched to a title, and spliced with backing audio. Nothing is ever posted anywhere until a human reviews it on our Final Check screen and explicitly approves it — there is no auto-posting path in this product.

04Scheduling & confirming the post is live

Once approved, the post moves to our existing scheduling queue, which already assigns a publish time per platform's posting cadence. At the scheduled time, our backend calls TikTok's Content Posting API to publish the video, then polls the Get Post Status endpoint and updates our own dashboard once TikTok confirms the post is live — the same "submit, then poll for confirmation before ever telling anyone it succeeded" pattern our storefront already uses for order fulfillment elsewhere in this codebase.

05Scopes we're requesting, and why

  • Login Kit (user.info.basic) — to authenticate our own TikTok account and confirm which account we're about to post to before every publish, per TikTok's own "show the creator's confirmed account" requirement.
  • video.publish — to publish an approved video directly to our TikTok account on the schedule our team sets.

We understand that until our app passes TikTok's compliance audit, every post made through the Content Posting API will be restricted to SELF_ONLY visibility, and we're building and testing against that constraint from day one — we're not requesting or expecting public posting ability before an audit.

06Compliance commitments

  • Every post is created and approved by a human member of our own team — there is no automated content generation or auto-posting.
  • We only ever post to our own connected TikTok account, never on behalf of a third party.
  • We display the connected account and a full content preview before every post, and require explicit approval each time.
  • We respect TikTok's per-account posting caps and will not attempt to exceed them.
  • We will not request scopes beyond Login Kit and video.publish unless our use of the product genuinely expands to need them.
